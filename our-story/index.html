Our Story

About Us

Mike-on-a-Bike

I’m Mike, known around here as “Mike-on-a-Bike.” Having been lucky enough to be born and raised here in Santa Barbara, I have come to love this town and its history, and sharing that love with visitors. After retiring from a corporate job I decided to do only things that I enjoy. After biking across the USA and many other bike adventures, I began American Riviera Bike Tours to continue my passion for exploration, history, and sharing this beautiful town.

Why is it called American Riviera? Come ride with us and find out. I look forward to making your biking experience easy, fun, and full of quirky local trivia.

My favorite bike is the regular cruiser. It’s an easy and fun way to get around town.

More on American Riviera Bike Tours

American Riviera Bike Tours was started in 2024 after I finally retired from my day job. I’ve given ~100 tours at my previous job.

Since I was in my early 20s, I’ve always wanted to have my own bike shop, and I knew it wouldn’t be fun unless I offered a comprehensive tour of my favorite city.

Today, I am proud to call myself the founder of an amazing company providing bike tours of Santa Barbara! Come pay us a visit and ask for Mike–I’d be happy to meet you!

Our Love for Tacos on our Tours

While you can’t go wrong on any tour or destination in Santa Barbara, we always recommend our guests take a stop at our recommended taco shops. Before you take off, ask one of our staff members about their favorite stop–you won’t be disappointed!

American Riviera Bike Tours and Rentals provides tours of Santa Barbara led by locals with rich knowledge and history of the area.
